Privacy & Consent Disclosure
Please review before continuing.

Subul IN processes data only when you take action (Analyze, Generate, Scan).

  • LinkedIn page data is parsed in your browser at your request.
  • Data is sent over HTTPS to the Subul backend and processed by Azure OpenAI (no model training).
  • Limited local caching in chrome.storage.local improves performance.
  • No background bulk scraping, no data sale, and deletion requests are supported.
